UAV Launcher

Bungee-powered launch rail for consistent, repeatable fixed-wing UAV take-offs.

Overview

Hand-launching heavier airframes quickly becomes the limiting factor in testing: it’s inconsistent, physically demanding, and hard to repeat when you are tuning power systems or running structured launch trials. The bungee-powered UAV launcher was our solution to that problem.

We started with a simple PVC-tube prototype to prove the concept, then moved onto a fully aluminium, field-ready launcher built around a V-slot rail. The first operational version was designed and built in under a month to meet a tight test schedule, and has since been iterated with telescoping legs, an improved dolly, and an automatic safety latch that locks the trigger when the launcher is loaded.

To size the bungee system we combined rough field testing with energy calculations to ensure we had enough stored energy for our target launch speeds. You can see how I approximated the required bungee strength and launch velocity in the bungee energy calculator write-up.

My contributions

  • Concept development and requirements: moving from hand-launch to a controlled, repeatable rail launch system.
  • Full CAD design of the launcher structure, dolly, trigger mechanism and safety latch.
  • Selection and integration of the 40 × 40 mm V-slot rail, wheels and eccentric hardware.
  • Analytical sizing of the bungee system and launcher stroke to meet target launch speeds for multiple airframe weights.
  • Machining and fabrication of aluminium plates, brackets, leg mounts and trigger components.
  • 3D printing of guards, spacers, cable guides and small functional parts for the launcher.
  • Assembly, static load checks and repeated field testing with instrumented launches.
  • Iterative refinements: telescoping leg geometry, safety latch behaviour and dolly alignment.
  • Documentation of launcher performance and integration into the broader UAV test workflow.

Components used

  • Main rail: 40 × 40 mm aluminium V-slot extrusion with matching V-slot wheels and eccentric nuts for preload.
  • Dolly / carrier: Aluminium plate and square-tube structure that supports the aircraft and interfaces with the V-slot wheels.
  • Legs: Aluminium square tubing and 20x20 v-slot rails for telescoping adjustment for levelling on uneven terrain.
  • Trigger & latch: Mechanical trigger mechanism with an automatic safety latch that locks when the launcher is loaded.
  • Bungee system: Multi-strand bungee cord sized for the required launch energy and draw length.
  • Hardware: Stainless fasteners, adjustable stops, tie-down points and ground anchors for field use.
