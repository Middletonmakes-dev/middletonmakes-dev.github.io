UAV Launcher

Bungee-powered launch rail for consistent, repeatable fixed-wing UAV take-offs.

Overview

Hand-launching heavier airframes quickly becomes the limiting factor in testing: it’s inconsistent, physically demanding, and hard to repeat when you’re tuning power systems or running structured launch trials. The bungee-powered UAV launcher was our way of taking that variable out of the equation.

We started with a simple PVC-tube prototype to prove the concept, then moved onto a fully aluminium, field-ready launcher built around a V-slot rail. The first operational version was designed and built in under a month to meet a tight test schedule, and has since been iterated with telescoping legs, an improved dolly, and an automatic safety latch that locks the trigger when the launcher is loaded.

To size the bungee system we combined rough field testing with energy calculations to ensure we had enough stored energy for our target launch speeds. The work-up includes measured draw weights at different extension lengths and an energy budget versus aircraft mass. You can see the details in the dedicated write-up: bungee energy calculation.

My contributions

  • Concept development and requirements: moving from hand-launch to a controlled, repeatable rail launch system.
  • Full CAD design of the launcher structure, dolly, trigger mechanism and safety latch.
  • Selection and integration of the 40 × 40 mm V-slot rail, wheels and eccentric hardware.
  • Analytical sizing of the bungee system and launcher geometry to hit target launch speeds for multiple airframe weights.
  • Machining and fabrication of aluminium plates, brackets, leg mounts and trigger components.
  • 3D printing of guards, spacers, cable guides and small functional parts for the launcher.
  • Assembly, static load checks and repeated field testing with instrumented launches.
  • Iterative refinements to telescoping leg geometry, safety latch behaviour and dolly alignment.
  • Documentation of launcher performance and integration into the broader UAV test workflow.

Components used

  • Main rail: 40 × 40 mm V-slot aluminium extrusion with matching V-slot wheels and eccentric nuts for adjustable preload.
  • Dolly / carrier: Machined aluminium plate and brackets that support the aircraft, interface with the V-slot wheels and keep the airframe aligned down the rail.
  • Legs: Aluminium square tubing with telescoping inner sections and adjustable feet for levelling on uneven terrain.
  • Trigger & latch: Mechanical trigger mechanism with an automatic safety latch that locks whenever the launcher is under tension.
  • Bungee system: Multi-strand bungee cord sized for the required launch energy and draw length, with scope to add or remove strands for different aircraft weights.
  • Hardware: Stainless fasteners, adjustable stops, tie-down points and ground anchors to make the launcher robust in the field.
